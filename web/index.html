Ontario Restaurant Kitchen Layout Designer

Free drag-and-drop designer by Dust 2 Engineering Inc., Mississauga. Real equipment templates with dimensions and supplier reference links, auto-sized Type I hoods (one per cooking line: equipment widths + 1 in spacers between equipment + 6 in at each end), and an equipment schedule you can download. Each hood is drawn on the plan as a gold dashed block: click it to select it, drag it to move it, rotate it or delete it, just like a piece of equipment. Lock a hood and it moves together with all of its cooking equipment as one group. Every item shows a typical reference price (or Not clear where no reliable price is published) and the schedule adds up an estimated equipment total. GTA permit drawings, flat fee $3,500 to $5,000, stamped by an Ontario P.Eng.
